Keys & tools

LOOK points to a square you can work out now. Free, but it counts as help.
CHECK shows which tiles do not fit.
REVEAL, then a square: shows the right number there.
PIN marks tiles you are sure about. It does not check them.
Undo takes back your last move. To send any tile back, tap it (on small squares: tap twice).

Stuck? Try a number in your head: if a neighbor breaks, it is not that number. Hard dailies and every level from 36 on need this.

Daily Challenge: one a day. Easy on Monday and Tuesday, hard Wednesday to Saturday, a big 7×7 on Sunday.

Keys: arrows or WASD move (ZQSD on AZERTY), 0–4 place a number, Backspace clears, U undo, P pin, C check, L look, R reveals the selected square.
